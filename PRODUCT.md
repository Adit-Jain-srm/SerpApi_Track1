# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Someone deciding whether to run a weekly AI-tool digest, then opening the guide to install it. They meet a short film first, not a dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Show a censored search resolving into one sample digest card, then send the reader to the usage guide. The film is the public face of the command-line digest. Success is a readable sample card, one next step, and a guide that can be followed without a secret on the page.

## Brand Personality

Exact, nocturnal, patient. The page should feel like a plate in a darkroom: most frames are still blocked, and one frame has come up.

## Anti-references

Cream SaaS landings, terminal-green consoles, editorial serif magazines, glass cards, and a row of equal hero buttons.

## Design Principles

1. Scroll is the pipeline. Forward resolves, backward censors.
2. The sample card repeats a real digest shape and says it is a sample.
3. One ceremony. Other links stay text.
4. The phone cut keeps one primary action and the same ending.
5. Reduced motion shows the resolved card, not a blank.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text at least 4.5:1. The hero has one keyboard-visible control. Reduced motion keeps the resolved still. The guide is a normal document with the same contrast.
